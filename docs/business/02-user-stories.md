# MHCS Business User Stories

**Status:** Candidate business authority — pending human approval

These stories define human intent and observable outcomes for the candidate
Messaging-led MHCS model. They do not prescribe UI, schemas, APIs, vendors, or
authentication mechanics.

| ID | Actor / Role | User Story | Acceptance Criteria | Traceability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-MEMBER-001 | Member | As a Member, I want to book, confirm, and receive care updates through the Messaging ecosystem, so that I can complete my healthcare journey without a permanent portal account. | B2B and B2C booking/status/reminder interactions can be coordinated through Messaging, with WhatsApp as the initial channel.<br>Requester, Payer, Subject of Care, Guardian, and Result Recipient remain distinct when relevant.<br>A booking code is a reservation locator, not clinical identity proof; on-site verification precedes check-in. | [Business Overview — Member journey](01-business-overview.md#member-journey)<br>[System Responsibilities — Member Core](03-system-responsibilities.md#member-core) |
| US-MEMBER-002 | Member | As a Member, I want to be notified when a result is finalized and open a temporary secure result surface when richer viewing or downloading is needed, so that I can access my result without a permanent member portal. | Finalization produces a Messaging notification.<br>A temporary result surface may support authorized view/download and returns the interaction to Messaging.<br>Raw NPZ and raw DICOM are not ordinary Member downloads. | [Business Overview — Member journey](01-business-overview.md#member-journey)<br>[System Responsibilities — Member Core](03-system-responsibilities.md#member-core) |
| US-STAFF-REG-001 | Site Staff — Reception / Registration | As Site Staff with the Reception / Registration role, I want to verify an arrival, confirm consent, and issue one visit ticket, so that the examination starts with the correct person and auditable registration evidence. | The staff member is eligible for the role and assigned to the site/shift.<br>The booking locator is checked against approved on-site identity evidence.<br>Signed paper consent is confirmed once, and one ticket advances the visit. | [Business Overview — Site Staff journey](01-business-overview.md#site-staff-journey)<br>[System Responsibilities — Operator Core](03-system-responsibilities.md#operator-core) |
| US-STAFF-EXAM-001 | Site Staff — Basic Examination | As Site Staff with the Basic Examination role, I want to claim the next eligible ticket and record the required basic examination, so that the Member can proceed to radiography with a complete operational record. | Eligibility derives from the held role, assignment, site, and shift.<br>FIFO claiming is atomic and only one staff member owns the active stage.<br>Required measurements, screening, and interview evidence are recorded before the ticket is released to radiography. | [Business Overview — Site Staff journey](01-business-overview.md#site-staff-journey)<br>[System Responsibilities — Operator Core](03-system-responsibilities.md#operator-core) |
| US-STAFF-RAD-001 | Site Staff — Radiography | As Site Staff with the Radiography role, I want to capture, review, and submit the complete radiograph set, so that the examination can be processed without mixing patient identity into offline capture files. | The staff member accepts an eligible assignment before opening a temporary Site Workspace.<br>Captures and matching gain input are reviewed, with retake/omission handling available before complete-set submission.<br>The active examination supplies identity association; capture files do not become an identity authority. | [Business Overview — Site Staff journey](01-business-overview.md#site-staff-journey)<br>[System Responsibilities — Operator Core](03-system-responsibilities.md#operator-core) |
| US-DOCTOR-RAD-001 | Doctor — Radiologist | As a Radiologist, I want to accept an eligible case offer and review it in a temporary Clinical / DICOM Workspace, so that I can make an accountable radiology decision without maintaining a permanent worklist portal. | Only cases matching professional, specialty, service, modality, and assignment eligibility are offered.<br>The case is claimed atomically and clinical work remains within the authorized workspace.<br>For radiology services, the Radiologist can record `usable` or `repeat_required` with a controlled reason/note. | [Business Overview — Doctor journey](01-business-overview.md#doctor-journey)<br>[System Responsibilities — Doctor Core](03-system-responsibilities.md#doctor-core) |
| US-DOCTOR-SPECIALIST-001 | Doctor — Authorized Specialist | As an Authorized Specialist, I want to accept and complete a case within my authorized specialty and service scope, so that clinical work reflects my professional authority without forcing radiology-only steps on me. | Offers and workspace access are limited by qualification, specialty, service, modality, and assignment eligibility.<br>The workflow supports the authorized specialty's clinical output.<br>Radiology quality and repeat-imaging decisions are not imposed unless separately authorized by the applicable clinical model. | [Business Overview — Doctor journey](01-business-overview.md#doctor-journey)<br>[System Responsibilities — Doctor Core](03-system-responsibilities.md#doctor-core) |
| US-ADMIN-001 | Global Admin / Super Admin | As a Global Admin / Super Admin, I want one persistent secure web surface over domain-owned administration, so that I can provision, configure, monitor, and resolve operational issues without fragmented admin actors or duplicated business domains. | Global Admin / Super Admin is one human actor and the only actor with a persistent conventional web login.<br>The surface manages domain-owned Member, Operator Core, Doctor, and Image Gateway capabilities while preserving their ownership boundaries.<br>Staff role eligibility, assignments, site configuration, and doctor authorization changes are auditable. | [Business Overview — Administration boundary](01-business-overview.md#administration-boundary)<br>[System Responsibilities — Unified Administration](03-system-responsibilities.md#unified-administration) |
