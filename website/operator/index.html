Site Staff · detailed user flow

One ticket, three concurrent service queues

01

Dispatch & workspace access

WhatsApp offer, temporary site workspace

Dispatch

Receive an eligible shift offer in WhatsApp

The offer names the operational role and site context without granting access by link possession.

Account gate

Are identity, permission, assignment, site, and time valid?

NoInactive or unassigned staff must contact an administrator.
YesAccept the offer and open the temporary Site Workspace for the assigned task.
Work context

Work only within an assigned permission

Reception / Registration, Basic Examination, and Radiography are independently assignable Site Staff roles. Station selection cannot grant or elevate a role.

History

Open `PEKERJAAN SAYA / RIWAYAT SAYA` in WhatsApp

Receive a concise assignment summary. Detailed inspection, when justified, opens a secure temporary Work History surface and returns to WhatsApp.

02

Ticket & queues

Site and shift order

Single ticket

Issue one site-and-shift ticket number

The same number follows basic examination & vital signs and radiography.

Eligibility

Is the member registered, eligible, and verified?

NoSend registration or payment back to Member Core.
YesContinue with paper informed consent.
Consent

Record the signed paper form

Store its version, signer, time, responsible staff, and required private scan. Downstream stations do not ask for consent again.

Ticket

Issue one site-and-shift number

The same number follows basic examination & vital signs, radiography, and result education.

Queue rule

Claim the next ready-time FIFO ticket

Claims are atomic. A reasoned skip requeues; recall keeps the same order.

03

Basic examination & vital signs assessment

Complete before radiography

Measurements

Record the required basic assessment

Temperature, height, weight, calculated BMI, and systolic/diastolic pressure require values or absence reasons.

Blood screening

Record three point-of-care results

Glucose, total cholesterol, and uric acid include units, sampling context, time, and method/device when relevant.

Short interview

Are all structured responses complete?

NoRecord an answer or allowed unavailable, refused, or not-applicable reason.
YesPreserve answers and optional notes as versioned patient-reported history.
Paper evidence

Is the approved paper questionnaire complete?

NoKeep basic examination incomplete until the completed paper questionnaire evidence is recorded privately.
YesRetain the completed paper questionnaire evidence privately before releasing the ticket.
Complete

Release the ticket to radiography

Basic examination & vital signs completion records its worker and makes the configured basic examination & vital signs earning eligible.

04

Radiography & AI waiting

Capture, submit, then release the station

Capture

Build and review one patient-free NPZ set

Use the active ticket identity, remove or retake poor captures, and submit the complete set once.

Gateway acceptance

Was the set durably accepted?

NoKeep the ticket in radiography service and retry safely with the same submission.
YesComplete radiography, make its stage earning eligible, queue background processing, and release the temporary workspace while Member and Doctor wait for the complete set.
Asynchronous AI

Release the Site Staff station

ReadyMember Core sends a WhatsApp notification and issues a secure temporary result link when required; the Site Staff workspace is not a member portal.
Desk LookupDesk staff can check the read-only monitor and print result on demand if patient asks.
05

AI Completion & LCD Display

Automated visit completion and LCD calling

Monitor & Print

Desk lookup and optional physical printout

Access published results via the AI Results Status Monitor for patient inquiries and optional on-demand printing.

Complete

Auto-close the visit

Ticket completes automatically when AI readiness is published. Doctor review remains a separate WhatsApp-coordinated request.

Public display

Pair a read-only LCD session

Show only Pemeriksaan Dasar and Sesi Foto Radiografi calls—for example A-002 → radiographic photo session and A-003 → basic examination & vital signs.

Site Staff restriction: no patient data on the LCD, no unassigned patient record browsing, no doctor reports, and no raw NPZ. Authenticated Site Staff whose active site, role, and current shift authorise the examination may view and download each returned raw DICOM as a standard `.dcm` attachment; it is not public and has no permanent public URL.
