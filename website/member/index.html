01

WhatsApp interaction

Persistent channel, no member portal

Decision

How does the interaction begin?

B2BThe business provides the agreed booking and funding context; MHCS notifies the requester or subject through WhatsApp.
B2CThe requester starts a booking conversation through WhatsApp and identifies the subject of care.
Channel

Continue in WhatsApp

Members do not receive a permanent username, password, portal, or application.

Validation

Is the request recognized and in scope?

NoUse human handoff or another approved recovery path; do not infer clinical identity from the chat alone.
YesContinue structured booking, notification, or result orchestration in WhatsApp.
02

Funding & booking context

Keep requester, payer, and subject distinct

B2B provisioning

Receive an appointment or start a booking

Member Core records the booking, payer, subject of care, guardian where relevant, and result recipient without creating member login credentials.

Required action

Confirm the structured details in WhatsApp

Confirm the service, subject, site, schedule, and payment or funding status through the approved conversation flow.

Wallet rule

Who is involved in the booking?

Requester / payerThe person communicating may be different from the payer and from the subject of care.
Guardian / recipientGuardian and result-recipient relationships are recorded separately where applicable.
Exception

Suspended account

Stop access and direct the member to support or an administrator.

03

Booking & attendance

Locator first, clinical identity on site

Booking source

Is this booking B2B or B2C?

B2BThe business determines the examination, result option, location, date, and shift.
B2CThe requester coordinates the examination and payment through WhatsApp.
Change control

Can the booking be located?

The booking code is a reservation locator only. It does not establish clinical identity or replace on-site verification.

Attendance result

Did the member attend?

No — B2BThe paid booking and quota remain consumed; attendance consequences belong to the business.
YesTU / Registration verifies the approved identity evidence on site before clinical check-in.
Handoff

Attend the examination

Walk-ins and additional bookings are registered and paid or authorized before operator confirmation.
