  1. Member Core confirms a business-assigned B2B booking or a WhatsApp-coordinated B2C booking and its approved funding or payment status.
  2. Reception / Registration Site Staff confirms registration and paper consent, issues one ticket, and Basic Examination Site Staff completes the required assessment.
  3. The same ticket enters X-ray, where Radiography Site Staff captures, reviews, and submits the image set.
  4. The MHCS Platform accepts and processes the complete image set.
  5. The complete images become available to the member independently of AI and doctor review.
  6. If AI analysis was selected, the AI Service generates a report; a ready result is notified to the Member, while a delay leaves the result surface pending without keeping the patient onsite.
  7. Member Core sends a WhatsApp result notification and issues a secure temporary result link when a richer view/download is required; doctor review remains a separate WhatsApp-coordinated request.
  8. If doctor review was selected, the doctor claims the study, reviews it, and submits a final report.
  9. The member can view each selected report independently before the current examination-service episode completes.
