Part 1 · Healthcare Landscape

The Healthcare Challenge in Indonesia

Across Indonesia's archipelago, preventive screening and diagnostic interpretation face structural geographic and specialist distribution bottlenecks.

01 Observed Gap

Fragmented Service Handoffs

Disconnection between patient booking, on-site check-in, vital measurements, X-ray capture, and diagnostic reporting causes lost records and steep drop-offs.

02 Structural Shortage

Specialist Shortage at Sites

Distributed Puskesmas and clinics have radiography equipment and operators, but lack on-site certified radiologists and specialist clinicians for timely interpretation.

03 Adoption Barrier

Citizen Engagement Friction

Forcing citizens to download proprietary mobile apps or manage portal logins causes steep drop-offs in preventive screening participation.

04 Governance Necessity

Safety & Consent Gaps

Clinical identity, guardian authorization for dependents, and signed paper consent must be physically verified on-site with high fidelity, not inferred online.

Part 2 · Platform Identity

What is MHCS: Orchestration, Not Monolithic Replacement

MHCS (Madeena Health Care System) is an Indonesia-led healthcare orchestration and coordination platform.

“Use existing capability; integrate where necessary; localise for the operating context; partner where appropriate; build only where capability is genuinely missing.”

— MHCS Guiding Product Principle

What MHCS Does

Core Orchestration
  • Coordinates care pathways across distributed Puskesmas, clinics, hospitals, staff, and doctors.
  • Manages member booking, on-site physical identity verification, and signed paper consent.
  • Connects on-site operators to remote certified radiologists for timely study interpretation.
  • Provides zero-login temporary result links directly to citizens through familiar messaging channels.

What MHCS Does NOT Do

Boundary Discipline
  • Does NOT replace healthcare professionals or clinical judgment (medical decisions remain solely with qualified clinicians).
  • Does NOT replace healthcare facilities (hospitals, Puskesmas, or clinics).
  • Does NOT replace PACS (Picture Archiving and Communication Systems).
  • Does NOT replace HIS/SIMRS (Hospital Information Systems) or EMR/RME (Electronic Medical Records).
  • Does NOT replace SATUSEHAT or other national health infrastructure.
  • Does NOT require citizens or staff to install heavy, proprietary mobile app stores.
Part 3 · Architecture

The Interaction Model: Messaging to Temporary Web

Durable product abstraction: Persistent human coordination through Messaging, backed by task-scoped Temporary Web Workspaces without permanent portal logins.

1. Messaging Interaction Surface

Persistent human touchpoint for appointments, dispatch offers, queue status, and notification links. Reference Indonesian channel: WhatsApp.

→

2. Temporary Secure Web

Time-limited, signed URLs opening task-focused workspaces: Member Temporary Result Surface, Site Workspace, Clinical / DICOM Workspace.

→

3. MHCS Orchestration Core

Coordinates workflow states, staged queues, image processing pipelines, access governance, and B2B/B2C financial accounting.

Member Surface

Engages via WhatsApp. Receives booking codes, directions, and secure time-limited result links. No username/password required.

Temporary Site Workspace

Shift-scoped interface for Reception, Basic Exam, and Radiography staff. Claims tickets, captures vitals, submits X-rays.

Temporary Clinical Workspace

Case-scoped DICOM viewer for remote Radiologists and Specialists. High-fidelity review, repeat requests, and report drafting.

Persistent Admin Web

Multi-domain governance, audit trails, B2B quota allocations, staff credentialing, and exception handling for administrators.

Part 4 · Focused Scope

The Current Service Slice: Examination to Result

The initial focused operational episode coordinates screening through result delivery, while preserving connectivity to longitudinal care.

Stage 01

Booking & Allocation

B2C citizen booking or B2B enterprise quota allocation via Messaging Interaction Surface.

Stage 02

Check-in & Consent

On-site photo identity verification and signed paper consent confirmation by Reception staff.

Stage 03

Basic Examination

Vital signs recording, point-of-care blood screening, and structured interview before X-ray.

Stage 04

Radiography Capture

Operator captures X-ray, reviews quality, retakes if justified, and submits study to Image Gateway.

Stage 05

Processing & AI

Controlled conversion, secure storage, and optional assistive AI analysis (fails closed if offline).

Stage 06

Result & Specialist

Temporary result delivery to member; optional remote Doctor review and formal radiology report.

Service Episode vs Healthcare Journey: An imaging service episode completes safely when the verified result is delivered. For members with notable findings, MHCS connects into downstream referral, specialized consultation, and continuous monitoring.

Part 5 · Value Creation

Stakeholder Value Proposition

MHCS delivers clear, tangible benefits across every participating group in the healthcare ecosystem.

Members (Citizens)

  • Frictionless WhatsApp booking & notifications.
  • Zero-login secure temporary result viewing.
  • Zero-cost repeat exam if doctor requests retake.
  • Verified on-site identity & guardian protection.

Healthcare Sites

  • Structured queue & staff station dispatch.
  • Connects existing X-ray equipment to specialists.
  • Digital coordination without replacing local HIS.
  • High throughput for preventive checkups.

Doctors & Specialists

  • Flexible remote case claiming anytime.
  • Browser-based high-fidelity DICOM workspace.
  • Clear per-study earnings tracking & payouts.
  • Retains complete diagnostic authority.

B2B Enterprise

  • Centrally funded employee screening packages.
  • Multi-location & shift quota management.
  • Live attendance tracking & usage reconciliation.
  • Healthier, proactive workforce preventive care.

Collaboration Partners

  • High-impact reach across Indonesian healthcare.
  • Clean modular architecture & API boundaries.
  • Localized for Indonesian health regulations.
  • Long-term continuous care roadmap.

Explicit Boundaries and Non-Claims

  • No Clinical Diagnostic Authority: Medical interpretation and clinical diagnostic conclusions remain solely with qualified licensed doctors. AI is purely assistive.
  • Healthcare Coordination Only: MHCS coordinates care journeys and workflow handoffs. It does not replace healthcare professionals or clinical judgment, healthcare facilities, PACS, HIS/SIMRS, EMR/RME, or SATUSEHAT national infrastructure.
  • No Speculative Commercial Claims: No fee schedules, pricing guarantees, revenue projections, or investment commitments are implied by this presentation.
  • No Premature Certification Claims: Regulatory clearance and production certifications are pursued through formal Indonesian authorities and are not claimed prematurely.
Part 8 · Ecosystem Partnerships

Collaboration Opportunities

MHCS is designed to help coordinate care across organizations by connecting local operational readiness with complementary technology and healthcare service partners.

Local Operational Readiness & Governance

  • Engineered for Indonesian Realities: Aligned with frontline healthcare workflows, Puskesmas realities, and nationwide health regulations.
  • Data Sovereignty & Privacy: Patient identification, physical consent, and clinical records respect national sovereignty boundaries.
  • Frontline Feasibility: Works with existing physical sites, radiography equipment, and messaging habits without forced operational disruption.

Potential Collaboration Models

  • Technology & Processing Integration: Connecting specialized imaging pipelines, processing intelligence, or assistive algorithms into care pathways.
  • Facility & Diagnostic Networks: Enabling hospitals, clinics, and specialist reading groups to participate in distributed clinical review.
  • Enterprise & Institutional Screening: Partnering with employers, insurers, or regional health initiatives for structured, measurable preventive health check-ups.
