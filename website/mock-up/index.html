PATIENT JOURNEY · MHCS OVERVIEW

MHCS Continuous Healthcare Journey Mockup v0.4

From healthcare capabilities to continuous health outcomes.

MHCS orchestrates existing healthcare capabilities into continuous end-to-end healthcare services.

Madeena Health Care System is an Indonesia-led healthcare orchestration and coordination system. It helps keep care moving until the required action happens and the intended health outcome is reached — and, where appropriate, maintained over time.

MI Made in Ina / Made in Indonesia — for the people of Indonesia.
THE POINT OF MHCS

Healthcare should not stop at screening, a result, data entry, or a referral.

The pathway continues toward the intended outcome. Monitoring can continue after recovery or control when health needs change over time.

MHCS coordinates the ecosystem; it does not replace it.

02 · CONTINUOUS HEALTHCARE
