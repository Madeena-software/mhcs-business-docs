Text equivalent and semantic notes

MHCS is an Indonesia-led healthcare orchestration and coordination system. Its current specified slice is examination, radiography, AI, and optional Doctor Review. Messaging is the persistent interaction layer. Specialized temporary surfaces appear for richer operational, clinical, or result work. Global Admin is the Persistent Admin Web exception; Member interaction remains Messaging-led without a permanent portal.

Operational demonstrator and the detailed journey pages remain available as companion views.
