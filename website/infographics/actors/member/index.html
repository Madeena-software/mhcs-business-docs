Member

Goal: coordinate care and receive member-safe outcomes.

Messaging → On-site / Physical → Temporary Result Surface → Messaging

Coordinate booking, payment/status, reminders, and optional Doctor Review in Messaging. Attend for identity verification and service delivery. Receive notification and open the Temporary Result Surface when richer viewing/download is needed.
